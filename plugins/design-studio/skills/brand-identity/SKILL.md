# Brand Identity

A brand identity is one idea expressed the same way everywhere. This skill gives the method from a client's first
answer to a system the website and social posts can follow, with the evidence for each step and real cases.

The guided, stage-by-stage session lives in the `studio` skill, which uses this one. The masters behind the logo
methods (Rand, Chermayeff & Geismar & Haviv, Bass, Glaser, Bierut, Scher, Hara) and their verified quotes live in
`design-history`. Audience, bias, and persuasion ethics live in `cognitive-psychology`. Looks and token recipes live in
`visual-styles`.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Strategy before form.** No logo before an approved concept, and no concept before an approved route. Each gate is
   approved in writing and logged with a date (`strategy-and-concept.md` §0).
2. **One concept: a sentence + a drawable metaphor + three expressions** (mark, system, motion). It must pass the swap,
   drawable, generative, durable, ownable, and truthful tests before the client sees it (`strategy-and-concept.md`
   §4.1).
3. **A symbol needs a reason.** Default to a wordmark. Add a symbol only when the typology procedure justifies it
   (`logo-design.md` §1.2): long name, icon-first product, sub-brands, or variety as the story.
4. **Three routes that differ in kind:** literal, abstract, typographic. Forty variations of one idea count as one
   (`logo-design.md` §2.4). No route before steps 2–6 of `logo-design.md` §2.2 are done and the thumbnail wall is
   shown; read §2.2–2.4 in full before starting.
5. **Smallest size and one color first.** Judge every route at 16 px and in solid black before color. Present every
   route with its test strip (`logo-design.md` §5.6, §6.1).
6. **The system derives from the logo's construction.** Color, grid, graphic device, and above all motion come from
   how the mark is built (`identity-systems-and-motion.md` §1.3–1.4, §4.2). Lock at least two elements on every
   touchpoint.
7. **Run the throughline test before delivery.** Every visible choice on every touchpoint traces to a core line
   (`identity-systems-and-motion.md` §5.5).
8. **Distinctive beats clever.** Recognition comes from unique, consistently used assets more than from hidden
   meaning (Romaniuk, graded [E2]; `strategy-and-concept.md` §3). Never promise that a mark alone will communicate the
   business. Meaning is earned over time (Rand, `logo-design.md` §0).
9. **A similarity check is not clearance.** Run the database and reverse-image checks, then tell the client in
   writing that legal clearance needs a trademark lawyer. The same applies to names.
10. **Evolve known marks; don't erase them.** List the equities people recognize before changing a known brand
    (Gap 2010, Tropicana 2009, Jaguar 2024; `logo-design.md` §4).
